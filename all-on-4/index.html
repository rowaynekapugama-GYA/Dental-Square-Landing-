The treatment

A full new smile on just four implants

All-on-4® replaces an entire arch of missing or failing teeth with a fixed, non-removable bridge anchored to as few as four implants, no more loose dentures, and far fewer implants than replacing teeth one by one.

Four implants support a full fixed arch
The bridge attaches securely to the implants
Fixed replacement teeth that don't move

Fewer implants, lower cost

Maximum teeth on minimum implants, a more affordable, less invasive approach.

Fixed teeth in days

A fixed provisional bridge is often fitted within 1–3 days of surgery.

Look & feel real

Better fit, comfort, aesthetics and confidence than removable dentures.

What to expect

Your All-on-4 journey

1

Plan & design

3D scans and Digital Smile Design map out the shape, size and colour of your new teeth.

2

Surgery, asleep if you prefer

Any failing teeth are removed and the implants placed, with a sleep-dentistry (IV sedation) option available.

3

Fixed teeth in 1–3 days

A fixed provisional bridge is attached to your implants within days, you leave with teeth that look and feel real.

4

Your final bridge

After healing (around six months) we fit your final, perfectly-adapted All-on-4 bridge, smile with confidence.
