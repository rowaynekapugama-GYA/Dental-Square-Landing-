Our cosmetic treatments

Enhance the colour, shape and aesthetics of your teeth

Whatever's holding your smile back, there's a treatment for it — and your free consultation will map out the right combination for you.

Porcelain veneers

Custom, wafer-thin porcelain restorations — our signature ultra-natural look, hand-crafted with Australia's best ceramists. Fix stains, gaps, chips and worn teeth.

From $1,950 per tooth*

Composite veneers & bonding

Hand-sculpted tooth-coloured resin to close gaps, repair chips and improve shape and colour — often completed in a single visit, and excellent value.

ZOOM!® teeth whitening

Philips ZOOM!® WhiteSpeed — up to 12 shades whiter in about an hour in-chair, or with a custom take-home kit.

In-chair $650 · take-home kit $400

Gummy smile treatment

Laser gum contouring completed with tremendous ease — excess gum is gently reshaped without bleeding or sutures, revealing more of your natural teeth.

Crowns & bridges

High-quality porcelain restorations that rebuild damaged teeth and allow natural, favourable healing of the gums.

Complete smile makeovers

A fully designed smile combining veneers, whitening, alignment (including Invisalign®) or implants — planned around your face, age and personality.
