Is Invisalign® right for you?

What clear aligners can correct

Invisalign® can treat a range of orthodontic concerns, from mild to moderate. Your free consultation will confirm whether it's suitable for your case.

Crowded teethMild to moderate crowding, gently unravelled.
Gaps between teethClose spaces for a fuller, even smile.
Protruding teethBring prominent front teeth back into line.
Crossbite correctionAlign teeth for a healthier bite.
Pre-veneer alignmentShort-course alignment before cosmetic work.
A healthier smileStraighter teeth are easier to clean and maintain.

How Invisalign® works

Your journey to a straighter smile

Using the latest CAD-CAM technology, a sequence of clear aligners is created, each slightly different from the last, moving your teeth a small increment at a time.

Free initial consultation

We assess your teeth, discuss your options and listen to what you want from your smile.

Digital scans

Accurate digital scans of your teeth are sent to the Invisalign® lab, where your custom aligners are made.

Wear & change

Wear each aligner set for about two weeks, top and bottom, with periodic check-ins to keep everything on track.

The new smile

By the end of treatment your teeth have moved into their new position, no metal wires or brackets involved.
