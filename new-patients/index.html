Modern, comfortable dentistry

The latest technology, for gentler visits

Our purpose-built West Ryde practice is fitted out to make your treatment easier, safer and more comfortable.

Laser technology

Treatments once considered invasive — like gum contouring and gummy smile treatment — can now be completed with tremendous ease, without bleeding or sutures.

Intra-oral camera

A tiny camera shows your teeth on the overhead monitor — so you can see exactly what we see, and understand your treatment. Patients love it!

In-chair TV entertainment

Ceiling-mounted TVs in every treatment room — watch your favourite movie, or let the kids enjoy cartoons, while you lie back and relax.

Low-dose digital X-rays

Around a third of the radiation of conventional X-rays, with sharper images we can enlarge on screen to catch even the smallest conditions early.

Amalgam-free & mercury-free

Only white, tooth-coloured, mercury-free fillings — with amalgam separators fitted to our plumbing to protect both patients and the environment.

ZOOM!® 1-hour whitening

Philips ZOOM!® WhiteSpeed — the world's #1 most requested whitening system, brightening teeth by up to 12 shades in about an hour.
