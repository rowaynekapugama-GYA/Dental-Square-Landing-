What are porcelain veneers?

Wafer-thin porcelain, life-changing results

Porcelain veneers are custom-made, wafer-thin tooth-coloured restorations bonded over the face of the tooth, among the most natural and aesthetic restorations in dentistry. They can be used to enhance a single tooth or transform your entire smile.

Stained or darkened teethBrighten discolouration that whitening can't fix.
Gaps between teethClose spaces for a fuller, even smile.
Chipped or misshapen teethRepair and reshape damaged edges.
Short or worn-down teethRestore length lost to wear and erosion.
Minor crowding or rotationsAn alternative to braces for select cases.

Your journey

See your new smile before we touch your teeth

After your smile design stage, porcelain veneers take just two appointments.

Free consult & smile design

We listen to how YOU want your smile to look, then design it, with 3D hand-crafted trial study models and an intra-oral "trial smile" in your mouth, plus extensive bite analysis, so you can preview the outcome before any treatment begins.

Preparation & trial veneers

Your teeth are lightly prepared and an impression taken. Provisional veneers mimicking the final result are placed for 1–2 weeks, so you can road-test your new smile.

The reveal

Your final porcelain veneers, custom made to your smile design, age, gender and preferred colour, are bonded using advanced bonding technology. The reveal is always the exciting part!
